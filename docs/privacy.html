Privacy policy

Effective 2026-11-19

Litematica Viewer is a free, open-source project run by an individual developer (albertchen857). This policy covers the website lite-viewer.com (including the web viewer and converter) and the desktop app. In short: your schematics stay on your device, we have no accounts, no ads and no tracking cookies.

1. Your schematic files

  • Files you open or convert in the web viewer / converter are read and processed entirely inside your browser. They are not uploaded to our servers or anyone else's.
  • When you drop a file on the home page, it is kept briefly in your browser's own storage so the viewer page can open it. The viewer also remembers the last file you opened in your browser storage, so you can reopen it with one click. This data never leaves your device and you can delete it by clearing the site data in your browser.
  • The desktop app works the same way: it only reads and writes files on your computer.

2. Opening a file from a link

If you paste a link (or use ?file=), your browser downloads that file directly from the other website. That website will see your request (for example your IP address) under its own privacy policy. The link is also shown in the address bar so you can share it — only share links you are happy for others to see.

3. Things stored in your browser

We use your browser's local storage only for preferences: colour theme, panel and tool settings, recently used items, and whether you have seen the tutorial. Your language choice is kept in the page address (?lang=). We do not set cookies.

4. Hosting, downloads and statistics

  • The website is hosted on Cloudflare Pages and the app downloads on Cloudflare R2. Like any web host, Cloudflare processes the technical data needed to deliver pages and protect the site (such as IP address and browser type) under the Cloudflare privacy policy.
  • We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to see how many pages are viewed and how fast they load. It does not use cookies, does not follow you across other sites and only gives us aggregated numbers — we cannot identify individual visitors.
  • The desktop app may download updated block textures from the project's GitHub repository. GitHub handles those requests under its own privacy policy.

5. What we do not do

No user accounts, no advertising, no selling or sharing of personal data, no profiling.

6. Other websites

Links to GitHub, Bilibili, YouTube, QQ and other sites lead to services with their own privacy policies.

7. Children

The project does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

8. Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. Questions: albertchen857@gmail.com.

  • 在网页版查看器 / 转换器里打开或转换的文件，全部在你的浏览器里读取和处理，不会上传到我们或任何人的服务器。

  • 我们用 Cloudflare Web Analytics 查看页面访问量和加载速度。它不使用 cookie、不跨站跟踪，我们只能看到汇总数字，无法识别具体访客。
  • 桌面版可能会从项目的 GitHub 仓库下载更新的方块贴图，这些请求由 GitHub 按其隐私政策处理。

5. 我们不做的事

没有用户账号、没有广告、不出售或分享个人数据、不做用户画像。

6. 其他网站

指向 GitHub、Bilibili、YouTube、QQ 等网站的链接，适用这些服务自己的隐私政策。

7. 未成年人

本项目不会有意收集任何人（包括未成年人）的个人信息。
